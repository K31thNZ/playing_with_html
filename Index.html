🕵️ Social Deduction Games New

The original game of Mafia was invented in Moscow in 1986 at Moscow State University. These games demand persuasion, listening, bluffing, and reading people — perfect English communication practice disguised as pure fun.

Mafia
★★★★★
8–20 players ₽500–700/person Weekly
The classic. Citizens vs. a secret Mafia — debate, argue, vote, and bluff your way to victory. Invented in Moscow, wildly popular internationally. A dedicated host/narrator is essential. Run with special characters (Doctor, Detective) for open events. Multiple rounds per session means high energy all night.
Werewolf / Ultimate Werewolf
★★★★★
8–35 players ₽500–700/person Weekly
Mafia's close cousin with rich role variety — Seers, Doctors, Hunters, Witches. Plays up to 35 people, making it ideal for large group events. One of the most widely recognised social deduction games globally. The "One Night" edition plays in just 10 minutes per round.
The Resistance: Avalon
★★★★★
5–10 players ₽500–700/person Weekly
No player elimination — everyone stays active all game. Knights vs. spies on quests. Considered one of the purest social deduction experiences: intensely strategic, conversation-heavy, and deeply replayable. Excellent for English practice as it demands precise, persuasive argument.
Blood on the Clocktower
★★★★★
7–20 players ₽700–1,000/person Bi-weekly
The gold standard of social deduction — richer roles, dead players still participate, and a Storyteller (you) shapes the game. Rated #1 on BoardGameGeek in its category. Premium experience: charge more, limit spots. Already played at ExpatEvents.org by the Moscow community.
Coup / Secret Hitler
★★★★☆
5–10 players ₽400–600/person Weekly
Coup: 15-minute bluffing card game — fast, ruthless, brilliant filler between larger games. Secret Hitler: Liberal vs Fascist factions across a political policy table — heavy on debate and persuasion, great for advanced English speakers. Both are highly repeatable in one evening.
Monetisation model: Charge ₽500–700/person for regular weekly games nights (Mafia, Werewolf, Avalon). Run premium one-off events (Blood on the Clocktower, Murder Mystery) at ₽800–1,200. Offer a monthly membership pass (e.g. ₽2,500/month for unlimited entry) to drive loyalty and predictable revenue. Social deduction nights are cheap to run — you own the games, the venue is a café — making them some of the highest-margin events in the schedule.
